01 · HOW IT WORKS

From stakeholder ask to shipped artifact.

Six steps. The same pattern whether the output is a deck, a PRD, a financial model, or a Webflow update. The discipline is what makes it repeatable.

STEP 01

Stakeholder ask

CEO, sales, marketing, finance, or eng describes the need in plain language. Voice memo, Slack DM, transcript, or a one-line prompt.

intake
STEP 02

Memory + research

Claude pulls from project memory, the Master Knowledge Base, Confluence, Drive, and BigQuery. Tier 1 library check first, then external sources.

3-tier research cascade
STEP 03

Skill resolution

The right Finexio skill loads: rfp-response, finexio-deck-gen, prd-pipeline, brand-voice, finance:variance-analysis. Each skill brings its own playbook.

14 Finexio skills
STEP 04

MCP execution

Skills call connected systems through MCP servers: Salesforce, Webflow, Atlassian, Gmail, Drive, Sheets, BigQuery, ClickUp, HubSpot.

12 MCP connections
STEP 05

Output draft

Deck, PRD, RFP response, financial model, blog post, Webflow update. Brand voice rules and Finexio positioning are applied automatically.

artifact
STEP 06

Audit, review, ship

Voice guard blocks slop. Layer 1 audit checks math. Layer 2 sub-agent runs adversarial review. Human approves. Then publish.

2-layer audit
